Questions patients ask.

Is my information private?

Accounts are email-verified, passwords can be reset by email and every form is protected against forged requests. Only you and your clinic team can see your record.

What if I can’t make my appointment?

Open your portal, reschedule to another available slot, or dismiss the notice. Your appointment history stays on record.

Do I need an account to book?

Yes. Creating one takes a minute: sign up, then confirm your email address. After that you can book, view your prescription and manage reminders.

Does the portal give medical advice?

No. It supports clinic operations only. It does not diagnose, prescribe or check clinical accuracy. Your eye care professional does that at your visit.
